Why do I need a power analysis?

If your experiment doesn't have a large enough sample, you might not be able to detect a meaningful effect, even if one exists. Power analysis helps you determine the right sample size to confidently identify whether your experiment's results are due to chance or a real effect. The power analysis tool has two modes:

  • Solve for sample size: Input your baseline conversion rate, minimum detectable effect, significance level, and desired power to calculate the required sample size per group.
  • Solve for MDE: Input your baseline conversion rate, sample size per group, significance level, and desired power to calculate the minimum detectable effect.
